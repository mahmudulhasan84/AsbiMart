1 Order Processing & Dispatch Schedule

  • Orders placed before 4:00 PM are packed and handed over to our delivery partner on the same day.
  • Orders placed after 4:00 PM or on public holidays are dispatched on the next working morning.
  • Every glass jar, honey bottle, and oil container is double-sealed with food-grade safety shrink-wrap and bubble-cushioned boxes to prevent leakage.

3 Cash on Delivery & Parcel Checking

You can place orders with Cash on Delivery (COD) without any advance payment for regular orders. Please keep your phone reachable so the delivery rider can coordinate your drop-off smoothly. You may verify the parcel seal and items in front of the rider before payment.
