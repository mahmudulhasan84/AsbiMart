Data Protection & Trust

Privacy Policy

AsbiMart respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share while browsing, registering, or ordering with us.

1 Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or contact our support team, we collect only the essential information required to serve you:

  • Full name, mobile phone number, and optional email address.
  • Shipping address (Division, District, Upazila/Thana, and House/Road details).
  • Order history, cart items, wishlist preferences, and customer support inquiries.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm, pack, ship, and deliver your organic food orders accurately.
  • To auto-fill your saved shipping address during checkout for faster ordering.
  • To provide live order tracking updates and responsive customer support.
  • To improve product availability, website security, and shopping experience.

3 Account & Data Security

Your account password is stored using cryptographic hashing (never in plain text), and authenticated sessions are protected with signed security tokens. We never sell, rent, or trade your phone number, address, or personal details to third-party marketers. Only the delivery name, phone number, and shipping address are shared with our courier partners (RedX, Steadfast, or Sundarban Courier) strictly to deliver your parcel.

4 Browser Storage & Cookies

AsbiMart uses browser local storage strictly to preserve your shopping cart, wishlist items, and signed-in session smoothly across pages. You can clear browser storage at any time from your browser settings.

5 Your Privacy Rights & Contact

You can update your profile details or password anytime from your Profile Dashboard. To request account or data removal, contact our privacy team at asby056@gmail.com or call 01821-296056.
